05About

I am a PhD researcher in Geological Engineering based in Beijing, working at the intersection of geotechnical engineering, geohazard science and artificial intelligence.

My background combines geotechnical experiments and physical-mechanism studies with field and engineering experience in geological-hazard investigation, slope engineering, structural monitoring and urban infrastructure inspection.

My current research focuses on scientific AI for complex geotechnical and geohazard systems, particularly how sparse and heterogeneous observations can be used to understand system dynamics, forecast long-term evolution and failure, and support trustworthy engineering decisions.

I am especially interested in connecting real-world observations, physical understanding and modern machine learning.
