03Data + Physics + Knowledge

Observations describe system states. Physical principles constrain plausible behavior. Domain knowledge connects material properties, environmental triggers and failure mechanisms. My developing research program asks how these sources of evidence can jointly support scientific learning of geosystem dynamics.
