Geoenvironment & spatial mapping

Distribution and environmental characteristics of expansive soils in China

IN THIS PROJECT

Research question and methodsResultsEngineering insightsRelated papers

Research question and methods

Existing national maps of expansive soils in China generally depict approximate extents, and internal differences within major distribution areas and their outer boundaries still require refinement at a consistent scale. This study integrates known occurrence records and multisource environmental information, combines spatial validation, repeated sampling, and model ensembles for national mapping, and compares the results with existing distribution boundaries and the intensity of research in the literature. The results show a principal distribution belt in central and eastern China, regional clusters in southwestern and southern China, and localized patches elsewhere, providing a basis for identifying undersurveyed areas and planning supplementary sampling.

Results

Engineering insights

Comparing the predicted distribution with existing boundaries and the intensity of research in the literature identifies areas where knowledge of the distribution is relatively consistent and areas where information is relatively limited, helping prioritize subsequent investigations of stratigraphy, mineral composition, and engineering properties.
