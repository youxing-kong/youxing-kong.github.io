Ongoing Research

Ontology-Guided KG-RAG for Geoscientific Knowledge and Reasoning

Connecting scientific literature, structured knowledge and reasoning

Research program
Learning Geosystem Dynamics with Data, Physics & Knowledge ↗

Research question

Can structured domain knowledge improve the reliability and scientific usefulness of AI reasoning in geohazard research?

Context & data

Scientific literature on geomaterials, swelling and shrinkage, environmental triggers, hydrological response, crack evolution, hydro-mechanical instability, monitoring, prediction, mitigation and risk management.

Approach

  1. Scientific literature
  2. Domain ontology
  3. Knowledge graph
  4. Retrieval
  5. LLM reasoning

Current findings & progress

Ongoing research organizes domain knowledge and source evidence to support retrieval and scientific reasoning.

RESEARCH UPDATE · 26 AUGUST 2026

The 26 August 2026 report describes an ontology-guided knowledge framework built from 536 full-text papers, evaluated using 170 domain questions and an expansive-soil landslide case. These are progress-report materials, not a claim of publication.

Scientific direction

Can Scientific AI jointly reason over data, physics and knowledge?

Next steps

Improve evidence traceability and the alignment of generated conclusions with retrieved scientific sources.

Outputs

Ontology, knowledge organization and evaluation are ongoing. Public paper, code and data are forthcoming.
