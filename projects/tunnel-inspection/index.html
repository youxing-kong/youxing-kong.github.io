Intelligent infrastructure inspection

Occlusion removal and image enhancement for tunnel linings

IN THIS PROJECT

Research question and methodsResultsEngineering insightsRelated papers

Research question and methods

Occlusions from pipelines, equipment, and other objects in tunnel-lining scans obscure surface textures and hinder the identification of visible defects such as water leakage. This study constructs paired data using real scans and occluding objects, removes occlusions and reconstructs lining textures through conditional reverse diffusion and local soft blending, and examines the effects of enhanced images on downstream defect detection. The results demonstrate texture and joint restoration under different occlusion conditions, as well as changes in water-leakage detection confidence following image enhancement.

Results

Engineering insights

The illustrated water-leakage target receives a higher prediction confidence after occlusion removal, indicating that image enhancement improves the visibility of defect features at this location and can provide clearer input for downstream detection.
