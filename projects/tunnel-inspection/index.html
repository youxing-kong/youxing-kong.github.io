Intelligent Analysis of Real-World Tunnel Inspection Data

Learning infrastructure condition from engineering inspection

Research program
Learning Geosystems from Sparse Observations ↗

Research question

How can AI extract reliable infrastructure-condition information from complex, noisy and partially occluded tunnel inspection data?

Context & data

Mobile laser scanning, grayscale tunnel imagery, seepage inspection, structural surface defects, point clouds and tunnel geometry.

Approach

  1. Image enhancement
  2. YOLO-based defect detection
  3. Generative image completion
  4. Point-cloud and image processing
  5. Structural parameter extraction

Current findings & progress

The CV lists research on seepage-defect recognition from grayscale tunnel cross-section scanning images, alongside engineering experience in mobile laser scanning and structural-parameter analysis.

Scientific direction

This work represents the transition between real-world engineering monitoring and data-driven AI research.

Next steps

Continue developing reliable condition-information extraction from complex engineering observations.
