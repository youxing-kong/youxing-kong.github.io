Published & Continuing

Multiscale Mechanisms of Fissured Expansive-Soil Failure

Connecting cracks, material behavior and slope instability

Research program
From Progressive Deformation to Failure ↗

Research question

How do cracks alter the mechanical behavior and failure mechanisms of expansive soils and slopes?

Context & data

Fissured expansive soils and slopes investigated through laboratory, model-scale and field observations. The CV records this research during 2020–2024, with continuing research directions.

Approach

  1. Geotechnical experiments and fracture mechanics
  2. DIC and electrical resistance monitoring
  3. Microstructural characterization
  4. Slope model testing
  5. Field investigation

Current findings & progress

Published work addresses fissure extension through fracture-mechanics tests and slope deformation under loading and irrigation. A study on sliding-surface microstructure is listed separately as a preprint in the CV.

Scientific direction

This research forms the Mechanics foundation of my current Scientific AI research. AI predictions should ultimately correspond to real material behavior, physical processes and failure mechanisms.

Next steps

Connect physical-mechanism understanding with scientific learning of geosystem evolution and failure.
