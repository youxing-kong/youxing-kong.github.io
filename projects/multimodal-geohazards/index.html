Structured geological information from field observations

Research program

Research question

Can multimodal AI extract structured geological information and support preliminary hazard interpretation from real-world field observations?

Context & data

Field photographs and geological observations from engineering and geohazard investigation scenarios.

Approach

  1. Field imagery
  2. Multimodal AI
  3. Structured information extraction
  4. Evaluation
  5. Hazard interpretation

Current findings & progress

Ongoing work emphasizes reproducibility, structured outputs, evaluation and domain consistency.

Scientific direction

Combining field imagery, spatial information, geological context and domain knowledge toward scalable geological investigation.

Next steps
