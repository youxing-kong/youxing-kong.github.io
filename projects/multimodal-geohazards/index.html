Intelligent geological investigation

Geological hazard surveys along roads using large multimodal models

IN THIS PROJECT

Research question and methodsResultsEngineering insightsRelated papers

Research question and methods

Geological hazard surveys along roads have accumulated extensive two-dimensional imagery, but organizing hazard characteristics and risk information and obtaining slope geometry still require substantial manual effort. This study introduces large multimodal models into field-image interpretation to extract hazard types, survey indicators, and risk levels step by step, and combines this process with image-based three-dimensional reconstruction to obtain slope geometry. A road-inspection demonstration presents hazard locations and levels along the route, while three-dimensional modeling adds representations of slope surfaces and geometry.

Results

Engineering insights

Large multimodal models organize field images into verifiable survey records, while photogrammetric three-dimensional reconstruction provides additional information on slope morphology and geometry. Together, they support geological hazard surveys along roads and spatial verification.
